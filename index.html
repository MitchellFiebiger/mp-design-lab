Artwork

Send us whatever you've got

Most shops ask you to upload a print-ready vector file. Most people don't have one — they have a logo their cousin made in 2019, a screenshot with a white box around it, or an idea they've never drawn. That's the part we actually do.

That's the "Lab" in MP Design Lab

We'll clean up what you send, redraw it if it needs redrawing, pick type that holds up at print size, and separate the colors. Then you get a mockup on the actual shirt. Design work is included in the shirt price — not a line item.

About

We started because nobody would print twelve shirts

[Owner name] has been [designing / printing] in the Twin Cities for [X years].

MP Design Lab exists for the orders that get turned away: twelve for a family reunion, six for a new landscaping crew, one for your dad's birthday. The big printers aren't built for those, and the online ones won't touch your artwork — they just print whatever you upload and let you find out later that it came out pixelated.

Every order gets a real mockup from a real person before it goes to press. If the design isn't right yet, we keep working on it until it is. That's the whole business.
